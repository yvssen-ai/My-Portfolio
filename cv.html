Solis — café & bakery ordering platform

  • Production ordering site serving the full menu out of PostgreSQL, with a React/Vite frontend and GSAP motion.
  • Checkout goes through a single server-side function: the browser submits item IDs and quantities and nothing else, and every price is resolved on the backend, so a manipulated price has nowhere to land.
  • Order tracking works without an account, through receipt tokens, and all currency is stored as integer piastres so rounding errors cannot accumulate.

Next.js · TypeScript · Supabase · PostgreSQL · RLS · PWA

Tailor Workshop — bespoke tailoring management system

  • An iPad-first progressive web app for a bespoke tailoring workshop: customer records, measurement profiles, garment configuration, B2B pricing, and production tracked through cutting, assembly, ready and store with QR and NFC.
  • The database is the security boundary — order tables are read-only to every client, and each mutation passes through a function that re-derives the tenant from the auth token and re-prices from the catalogue, so a client-supplied price is simply ignored.
  • Composite foreign keys mean a row cannot reference another workshop's data even with the row-level security policies switched off, and money is stored as integer minor units rather than floats.
